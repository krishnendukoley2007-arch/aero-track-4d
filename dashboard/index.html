📖 Plain-English Concepts for Reviewers
Extreme Forecast Index (EFI):

An EFI-inspired z-score measuring how anomalous a forecast timestep is compared to the pre-onset ERA5 baseline (first 36 hours of the event window, representing ambient pre-cyclone conditions). A z-score > +2.0σ signals an extreme outlier departure from that baseline.

Spectral Smoothing Problem:

Standard deep learning models (CNNs and U-Nets) trained with Mean Squared Error (MSE / L2 loss) predict the mathematical mean $\mathbb{E}[Y|X]$. This averages out spatial variance and blurs the high-velocity eyewall winds that emergency responders need to track.

CorrDiff Generative Diffusion:

A two-stage generative model combining a multi-scale U-Net for the synoptic mean with a conditional score-based diffusion model. By stochastically generating realistic high-frequency turbulence, it restores the theoretical $k^{-5/3}$ Kolmogorov kinetic energy spectrum.

Spherical Geodesic Mesh:

Maps atmospheric fields directly onto an icosahedral geodesic mesh ($V=162$, $E=480$). Propagates anomalies across spherical neighbors using great-circle distance weighting and calculates 3D Cartesian centroids, eliminating planar polar distortion.

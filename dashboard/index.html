Plain-English Concepts for Reviewers
Extreme Forecast Index (EFI) & Shift-of-Tails:

Authentic non-parametric ECMWF integral comparing the forecast CDF against the pre-monsoon climatology CDF. SOT evaluates the departure of extreme percentiles (Q99 vs Q90) to isolate catastrophic outliers.

Spectral Smoothing Problem:

Standard deep learning models (CNNs and U-Nets) trained with Mean Squared Error (MSE / L2 loss) predict the mathematical conditional expected mean E[Y | X]. This averages out spatial variance and blurs the high-velocity eyewall winds that emergency responders need to track.

CorrDiff Generative Diffusion:

A two-stage generative model combining a multi-scale U-Net for the synoptic mean with a conditional score-based diffusion model. By stochastically generating realistic high-frequency turbulence, it restores the theoretical k-5/3 Kolmogorov kinetic energy spectrum.
